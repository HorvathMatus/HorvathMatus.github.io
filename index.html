I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Finance, Faculty of Economics and Administration, Masaryk University in Brno. My research focuses on portfolio theory and financial risk measurement — tail risk, Expected Shortfall and higher moments — with a particular interest in cryptocurrency and digital-asset markets. I also work on ESG, investor attention and cross-market spillovers.

I received my Ph.D. in Finance from Masaryk University in 2025 (summa cum laude). I have been a postdoctoral researcher with the IDA Institute of Digital Assets and the Institute of Economic Research of the Slovak Academy of Sciences, and a visiting researcher at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin and University College Dublin.
